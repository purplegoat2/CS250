# CS250

How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
- To implement user needs into a program, I think it is important to understand not only what users want but also why they need a feature. Creating User Stories is especially helpful because they explain the features users want and the reasons they need them from the user's point of view. User Stories can also help team members better understand the features, how they should work, and share the same goals.
  
How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
- When developing a program, I think it is more effective to divide the work into smaller parts and develop and test them one at a time instead of trying to complete everything at once. This makes it easier to find and fix problems during development. It also makes it easier to respond to new requirements or feedback. In my future development work, I plan to use Agile processes such as Sprint Planning to set priorities, Sprint Review to check the results, and Retrospectives to find ways to improve the development process.
  
What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
- I think being a good team member starts with seeing yourself as part of a team instead of working only as an individual. If I do not complete my work responsibly, it can affect the entire team and cause delays in the development schedule. I also learned through this course that communication between team members is very important. I believe that respecting other people's ideas, helping each other, and building good teamwork can help the team develop a better program.
